- 2026-10-17 (wavy-lyric root fix - "the standard way" per the user's own direction - **commit e6485907**): after two snap iterations both came back "wavy again" on the user's screen, the call was made to stop patching the legacy per-glyph pipeline and do what the user asked for: the standard font-engine path. **Why snapping could never win:** the waviness has TWO independent per-glyph error sources, and snapping only touches one. (1) independent per-glyph quad-corner rounding - fixed by snapping; but (2) every glyph was rasterized SEPARATELY at a fixed max height, its ink located with an INTEGER pixel scan, cropped into its own texture, then resampled at the real size - so each glyph carried a baked-in subpixel offset of up to ~0.4px (measured on FreeSans "singing") sitting in the texture itself, invisible to and unreachable from any quad-level correction. That per-glyph texture + integer-ink-scan + max-res-downsample design is the net48-era relic (Vocaluxe originally consumed pre-authored per-theme glyph sheets; the S4 runtime-raster port reproduced its shape). **Fix - the standard way:** rasterize the WHOLE line at (at least) its final size with Skia's font engine (SKCanvas.DrawText, the exact pattern the cover generator already uses) into ONE texture, then draw ONE quad. Per-glyph drift disappears by construction: the font engine lays out every glyph at subpixel precision relative to its neighbors in a single pass, and the one uniform quad resample preserves all of those relative positions exactly. The only remaining quantization is the single quad's corner rounding in COpenGL._DrawTexture, which shifts the WHOLE line by <0.5px - it can never move the letters relative to each other. CFonts.DrawText/DrawTextReflection/DrawText(begin,end) all draw the single line texture; GetTextWidth/GetTextHeight keep the legacy advance-based line-box convention (kerning now included) so theme layouts and centering are unchanged.
